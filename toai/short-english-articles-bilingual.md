# 英语短文阅读：生活、观点与科普

> 共 20 篇短文。每篇英文约 300–350 词，分段呈现，并在每段英文后附上对应中文翻译。建议先读英文，再用中文核对理解。

## 01. How AI May Change Our Future

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Artificial intelligence is already part of ordinary life. It recommends music, checks spelling, translates messages, and helps people find information. These tools may become more capable in the future, but their greatest effect may not be replacing every human worker. Instead, AI is likely to change how many jobs are done. A nurse may use it to organize patient records, while a designer may ask it to produce several early ideas. In both cases, the person still needs to judge what is useful, safe, and suitable for a real human being.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
人工智能已经成为日常生活的一部分。它会推荐音乐、检查拼写、翻译信息，也会帮助人们查找资料。未来这些工具可能会变得更强大，但它们最大的影响或许不是取代所有劳动者，而是改变许多工作的完成方式。护士可以用它整理病历，设计师可以让它生成几个初步构想。在这两种情况下，人仍然需要判断哪些内容有用、安全，并且适合真实的人。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Some tasks will probably disappear, especially repetitive work that follows clear rules. At the same time, new jobs will appear in areas such as AI training, data protection, and technical support. Workers may need to learn new skills more than once during their careers. Schools can help by teaching students how to ask good questions, check facts, solve problems, and work with others. These abilities matter because a computer can produce an answer quickly, but it cannot always understand a person's needs or the consequences of a decision.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
有些工作可能会消失，尤其是那些遵循明确规则的重复性工作。同时，人工智能训练、数据保护和技术支持等领域也会出现新岗位。人们在职业生涯中可能不止一次需要学习新技能。学校可以通过教学生提出好问题、核实事实、解决问题和与他人合作来提供帮助。这些能力很重要，因为电脑虽然能迅速给出答案，却不一定理解一个人的需求或某个决定带来的后果。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
The future will depend on the choices people make now. Companies should explain when AI is being used and protect the information they collect. Governments need rules that reduce harm without blocking useful research. Ordinary users also have a role: they should question surprising claims and avoid sharing private details with untrusted services. AI can help doctors notice patterns, make public transport more efficient, and give students extra practice. However, it should support human judgment rather than replace responsibility. A better future is not simply one with more powerful machines; it is one in which technology helps more people live and work well. People will also need clear ways to appeal decisions when automated systems make mistakes, especially when jobs, loans, or health care are involved.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
未来会受到当下选择的影响。企业应说明何时使用了人工智能，并保护收集到的信息。政府需要制定规则，在减少伤害的同时不妨碍有益的研究。普通用户也有责任：对令人惊讶的说法保持质疑，不要把私人信息分享给不可信的服务。人工智能可以帮助医生发现规律、提高公共交通效率，也能为学生提供额外练习。不过，它应该辅助人的判断，而不是取代人的责任。更好的未来并不只是拥有更强大的机器，而是让技术帮助更多人更好地生活和工作。当自动系统出错时，人们还需要明确的申诉渠道，尤其是在工作、贷款或医疗保健受到影响的情况下。

## 02. A First Trip to the United States

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
A trip to the United States can include busy cities, quiet parks, and long drives between them. Before leaving, check that your passport and travel permission are valid, and keep copies of important documents in a separate place. The country is large, so do not plan to visit too many distant cities in a few days. A flight from New York to Los Angeles takes several hours, and driving between them takes much longer. Choose a region and give yourself enough time to enjoy it instead of spending the whole trip in transit.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
去美国旅行，可以体验繁忙的城市、安静的公园，以及城市之间漫长的车程。出发前要确认护照和旅行许可有效，并把重要文件的副本另外保存。美国幅员辽阔，不要计划在几天内游览太多相距很远的城市。从纽约飞往洛杉矶需要几个小时，开车则要久得多。最好选定一个区域，留出足够时间慢慢游览，而不是把整个旅程都花在路上。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Daily habits may feel different from those at home. People usually leave a tip at restaurants, often around fifteen to twenty percent when service is not already included. Prices on menus may not include sales tax, so the final bill can be higher than expected. In many places, a car is useful, but city centers may have expensive parking and heavy traffic. If you rent one, learn the local road rules and keep your license and rental papers with you. In large cities, public transport and walking can be easier than driving.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
日常习惯可能和家乡有所不同。在餐馆用餐通常要给小费；如果服务费没有包含在账单里，小费一般约为百分之十五到二十。菜单上的价格可能不含销售税，所以最终账单会比预期高。在许多地方，汽车很方便，但市中心停车费可能很贵，交通也很拥堵。如果租车，要了解当地交通规则，并随身带好驾照和租车文件。在大城市里，乘坐公共交通和步行有时比开车更方便。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
For safety, keep your phone charged and know how to contact your hotel. Do not leave bags visible in a parked car, and stay aware of your surroundings, especially at night. The emergency number for police, fire, or medical help is 911. It is also wise to check opening hours before visiting museums or national parks because some places require advance reservations. Most importantly, leave room for surprises. Ask locals for food suggestions, take breaks when you are tired, and be respectful of different communities. A relaxed plan often leads to a richer trip than a long list of attractions. Carry a small amount of local currency, but use a bank card for most purchases and tell your bank about your travel plans.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
为了安全起见，要保持手机有电，并记得如何联系酒店。不要把包留在停放车辆里显眼的位置，尤其在夜间要留意周围环境。报警、火警或医疗急救电话是 911。参观博物馆或国家公园前，最好先确认开放时间，因为有些地方需要提前预约。最重要的是给意外发现留出空间。可以向当地人打听美食建议，疲倦时及时休息，并尊重不同的社区。与其安排一长串景点，不如制定轻松的计划，往往能收获更丰富的旅程。可以带少量现金，但大多数消费可以使用银行卡；出行前也要告知银行你的旅行安排。

## 03. E-books and Paper Books

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
E-books are easy to carry. A single device can hold hundreds of novels, travel guides, and language books, which is helpful on a long journey or in a small home. Readers can change the font size, search for a word, and often buy a new title within minutes. Many digital books are also less expensive than printed copies. However, reading on a screen can bring distractions. Messages, games, and websites are only a tap away, and some people feel tired after looking at a bright display for a long time.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
电子书携带方便。一台设备就能存放数百本小说、旅行指南和语言学习书籍，这对长途旅行或居住空间较小的人很有帮助。读者可以调整字体大小、搜索单词，而且通常几分钟内就能买到新书。许多电子书也比纸质书便宜。不过，在屏幕上阅读容易受到干扰。信息、游戏和网站只需轻轻一点就会出现，有些人长时间看着明亮的屏幕也会感到疲劳。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Paper books offer a different kind of comfort. Their pages have a familiar feel, and readers can see how much of the book they have finished. It is easy to mark a sentence, add a note, or lend a copy to a friend. A printed book does not need a battery or an internet connection, so it works almost anywhere. For some readers, turning pages makes it easier to focus and remember what they have read. On the other hand, books take up space, can be heavy, and may cost more to print and ship.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
纸质书带来另一种舒适感。书页触感熟悉，读者也能直观看到自己读完了多少。标记句子、写下笔记或把书借给朋友都很方便。纸质书不需要电池或网络，所以几乎在哪里都能阅读。对一些读者来说，翻页能让他们更容易集中注意力并记住所读内容。另一方面，书会占用空间，携带起来可能很重，印刷和运输成本也可能更高。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
There is no need to choose only one format. A student might use an e-book to search quickly through a textbook and keep a paper notebook for personal reflections. Someone who travels often may read novels on a tablet but keep favorite cookbooks in the kitchen. The best choice depends on the reader, the book, and the situation. What matters most is not the device but the time spent reading and thinking. If a format makes it easier for you to return to a book regularly, that is probably the right format for that part of your life. Libraries also provide digital books for free, giving readers a chance to try both formats before spending money.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
人们不必只选择一种形式。学生可以用电子书快速搜索教材内容，再用纸质笔记本记录个人想法。经常旅行的人可以在平板电脑上读小说，同时把喜欢的食谱书放在厨房里。最合适的选择取决于读者、书籍和具体场景。最重要的不是设备，而是花在阅读和思考上的时间。如果某种形式能让你更容易定期拿起一本书继续读，那它可能就是适合你当前生活的选择。图书馆也会提供免费的电子书，让读者有机会在花钱之前体验这两种阅读形式。

## 04. Building a Small English Habit

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Many people begin learning English with a big plan. They promise to study for two hours every night, memorize fifty words a day, and finish a thick grammar book in a month. Such goals can feel exciting at first, but they are difficult to keep when work becomes busy or family needs attention. Missing one day may then feel like failure, and a learner may stop completely. A smaller plan is often stronger. Ten focused minutes each day can build a real habit, especially when the activity is simple enough to begin without a lot of preparation.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
许多人开始学英语时会制定一个很大的计划。他们保证每天晚上学两个小时、每天背五十个单词，并在一个月内读完厚厚的语法书。这样的目标一开始令人兴奋，但工作忙起来或需要照顾家人时，就很难坚持。漏学一天可能会让人觉得自己失败了，甚至彻底停下来。更小的计划往往更牢靠。每天专注学习十分钟也能形成真正的习惯，尤其当活动简单到不需要太多准备时。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Choose one activity that you enjoy and connect it to a regular part of your day. You could listen to a short podcast while making breakfast, read one paragraph on the bus, or review five useful phrases before bed. Try to use the language, not only collect information about it. After learning a phrase, write a sentence about your own life and say it aloud. For example, after learning “run out of,” you might say, “We ran out of coffee this morning.” A personal example is easier to remember than a list with no connection to you.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
选择一项自己喜欢的活动，并把它和每天固定的生活环节联系起来。你可以在做早餐时听一段短播客、在公交车上读一个段落，或睡前复习五个实用短语。尝试使用英语，而不只是收集相关知识。学会一个短语后，可以写一个关于自己生活的句子，再大声说出来。例如，学到 “run out of” 后，你可以说：“We ran out of coffee this morning.”（我们今天早上咖啡喝完了。）和自己有关的例句比毫无关联的单词列表更容易记住。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
It also helps to review old material. A phrase may seem familiar today and disappear from memory next week if you never meet it again. Keep a small list of sentences you want to reuse, and look at it every few days. When you miss a study session, simply return the next day instead of trying to punish yourself with a much longer lesson. Progress is rarely a straight line. Some weeks will be easier than others, but a flexible routine can survive change. Over time, short daily practice adds up to many hours of listening, reading, and speaking. You can also record yourself once a week, because hearing your own improvement can make the practice feel rewarding.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
复习旧内容也很有帮助。一个短语今天看起来很熟悉，如果之后从未再遇到，到了下周可能就会从记忆中消失。可以把想重复使用的句子记在一个小清单上，每隔几天看一遍。错过一次学习时段也没关系，第二天继续就好，不必用更长的课程惩罚自己。进步很少是一条直线。有些星期会比较轻松，有些则不然，但灵活的日常安排更能适应变化。久而久之，每天的短时间练习会累积成许多听、读、说的小时数。你也可以每周录一次自己的声音，因为听到自己的进步会让练习更有成就感。

## 05. Making a City Easier to Walk In

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Many people use a car because it seems fast and convenient. Yet when every trip depends on driving, streets become crowded, parking takes time, and the air becomes less clean. A walkable city gives residents other choices. Shops, schools, parks, and bus stops are close enough to reach on foot. This does not mean that everyone must walk everywhere. It means people can choose a short walk for a nearby task instead of starting a car. That small choice can make daily life calmer and help neighbors notice one another.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
许多人开车出行，因为这看起来快捷又方便。然而，如果每段路都依赖汽车，街道就会变得拥挤，停车也要花时间，空气质量还会下降。适合步行的城市能给居民更多选择。商店、学校、公园和公交站都近到可以步行到达。这并不意味着所有人都必须走路，而是说去附近办事时，人们可以选择步行，而不必每次都发动汽车。这种小小的选择能让日常生活更从容，也让邻里之间有更多机会彼此照面。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Good sidewalks are only the beginning. People need safe crossings, street lights, benches, and trees that provide shade in summer. A road should be easy to cross for a child, an older person, and someone using a wheelchair. Drivers also need clear signs and slower speeds near homes and schools. Local businesses can benefit when people pass by on foot, because walkers may stop for a snack or visit a small shop. When streets feel welcoming, families are more likely to spend time outside rather than staying indoors.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
好的步道只是第一步。人们还需要安全的人行横道、路灯、长椅，以及能在夏天遮阴的树木。道路应该让儿童、老人和使用轮椅的人都能轻松通过。在住宅区和学校附近，司机也需要清楚的标志和较低的车速。当地商家也可能从步行环境中受益，因为路过的人也许会停下来买点心或逛逛小店。当街道让人感到舒适时，家庭就更愿意到户外活动，而不是一直待在室内。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Building a walkable city takes planning and money, so changes often happen one street at a time. Residents can help by telling local leaders where crossings feel unsafe or where a bus stop needs a bench. Cities can test a new design with temporary paint and barriers before rebuilding a road. The results should be measured: Are there fewer crashes? Do more people walk to nearby places? Are local shops doing well? A city is not improved by appearance alone. The best streets make daily needs easier to meet, reduce danger, and give people a pleasant place to share.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
建设适合步行的城市需要规划和资金，因此改变往往要一条街一条街地进行。居民可以告诉地方管理者哪些路口不安全，或哪个公交站需要长椅，从而提供帮助。在重修道路前，城市可以先用临时标线和隔离设施测试新设计。之后还应该衡量效果：交通事故是否减少？步行前往附近地点的人是否增加？当地商店经营得怎么样？城市不能只靠外观改善。最好的街道能让日常需求更容易得到满足，降低危险，也为人们提供舒适的共享空间。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Walking can also improve health without requiring a special gym or expensive equipment. A short walk after lunch may help someone feel more alert, and a walk with a neighbor can turn exercise into a social activity. Cities should make this choice possible for people of different ages and abilities, not only for fast, confident walkers.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
步行还能改善健康，而且不需要专门的健身房或昂贵的器材。午饭后短暂散步可以让人更清醒，和邻居一起散步则能把锻炼变成社交活动。城市应该让不同年龄和行动能力的人都能选择步行，而不仅仅是方便走得快、走得自如的人。

## 06. Finding Balance When Working from Home

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Working from home can save commuting time and make it easier to handle small tasks during the day. A parent may be able to meet a child after school, while another worker can start early and finish before evening traffic. But the same flexibility can make work feel endless. A laptop on the kitchen table may invite someone to answer one more message during dinner. Without a clear boundary, people can feel that they are always at work, even when the office is only a few steps away.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
在家工作可以节省通勤时间，也更容易在白天处理一些小事。家长也许能在放学后接孩子，另一些人则可以早点开始工作，在晚间交通高峰前结束。但同样的灵活性也可能让工作变得没有尽头。放在厨房桌上的笔记本电脑，可能会让人忍不住在晚饭时再回复一条消息。如果没有清晰的界限，即使办公室就在几步之外，人们也可能觉得自己一直在工作。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
A helpful step is to create a simple routine. Choose a regular start time, get dressed, and decide where work will happen. A separate room is ideal, but even one corner of a table can become a work area. At the end of the day, close the computer and put work materials away. Short breaks matter too. Standing up, stretching, or taking a brief walk can refresh the mind after a long video call. Workers should also tell family members when they are available and when they need quiet time.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
一个有帮助的做法是建立简单的日常安排。选定固定的开始时间，穿好衣服，并决定在哪里工作。独立房间当然理想，但即使只是桌子的一角，也可以作为工作区域。一天结束时，关上电脑并收起工作用品。短暂休息也很重要。长时间视频会议后，站起来、伸展身体或短暂散步，都能让头脑重新清醒。员工也应该告诉家人，自己何时方便交流，何时需要安静。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Employers have a role in making remote work healthy. They should set reasonable expectations about response times and avoid measuring effort only by the number of hours a person appears online. Teams can agree on meeting-free periods so people have time for focused work. At home, workers may need to communicate more clearly because they cannot rely on a quick conversation in the hallway. The goal is not to copy an office into the living room. It is to use flexibility in a way that supports good work and a full life outside work.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
雇主也有责任让远程工作保持健康。他们应该对回复时间提出合理要求，避免只根据一个人在线时长来衡量付出。团队可以约定不安排会议的时段，让大家有时间专注工作。在家办公时，员工可能需要更清楚地沟通，因为他们不能像在办公室那样在走廊里快速聊几句。目标不是把办公室原样搬进客厅，而是合理利用灵活性，让工作质量和工作之外的生活都得到支持。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Remote workers should also remember that isolation can build slowly. A regular lunch with a colleague, a shared online break, or a few hours in a library can provide human contact. A healthy arrangement is different for every person, so teams should talk openly about what helps them do their best work.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
远程工作者也要记得，孤独感可能会慢慢累积。和同事定期一起吃午饭、在线上共同休息一会儿，或去图书馆工作几个小时，都能增加人与人之间的交流。每个人适合的工作方式都不同，因此团队应该坦诚讨论哪些安排最有助于大家做好工作。

## 07. Why Sleep Is Part of a Healthy Life

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Sleep is not wasted time. During the night, the body rests, the brain sorts through information, and the immune system continues to work. People who regularly sleep too little may find it harder to pay attention, control their mood, or make careful choices. One bad night is usually manageable, but a long pattern of poor sleep can affect health and daily performance. Adults do not all need exactly the same number of hours, so it helps to notice how you feel after different amounts of rest.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
睡眠并不是浪费时间。夜间，身体得到休息，大脑整理信息，免疫系统也继续运作。经常睡眠不足的人可能会发现自己更难集中注意力、调节情绪或谨慎做决定。偶尔一晚没睡好通常还能应付，但长期睡眠不佳会影响健康和日常表现。成年人所需的睡眠时长并不完全相同，因此留意不同休息时长后的身体感受会很有帮助。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
A steady schedule can make it easier to fall asleep. Going to bed and waking up at similar times teaches the body when to rest. A quiet, dark, and comfortable room also helps. Some people sleep better when they avoid heavy meals, nicotine, and strong coffee late in the day. Phones and tablets can keep the mind active, so putting them aside before bed may help. If worries are keeping you awake, write down the next day's tasks and set the list aside until morning.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
稳定的作息能让入睡更容易。每天在相近的时间上床和起床，可以让身体知道何时该休息。安静、黑暗且舒适的房间也有帮助。有些人发现，傍晚后避免吃得太多、摄入尼古丁或喝浓咖啡，睡眠会更好。手机和平板可能让大脑保持活跃，因此睡前把它们放到一边也许会有帮助。如果忧虑让你无法入睡，可以把第二天要做的事写下来，先放到一边，等早上再处理。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Not every sleep problem can be solved by changing a bedtime routine. Pain, stress, shift work, medication, or a health condition may be involved. If someone often struggles to sleep or feels extremely tired during the day, speaking with a health professional is sensible. It is also helpful to avoid treating sleep as a competition. ## 14. How Renewable Energy Is Changing

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replaced, such as sunlight, wind, and moving water. Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity, while wind turbines use moving air to spin a generator. These technologies have become more common as equipment has improved and costs have fallen. They can reduce the need to burn coal, oil, and gas, which release pollution and heat-trapping gases. However, renewable power is not a single simple solution. Each place must consider its weather, geography, existing power lines, and the needs of the people who live there.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
可再生能源来自能够自然补充的资源，例如阳光、风和流水。太阳能板把阳光转化为电力，风力涡轮机则利用气流带动发电机旋转。随着设备改进、成本下降，这些技术变得越来越普遍。它们可以减少对煤炭、石油和天然气的依赖，而燃烧这些燃料会释放污染物和温室气体。不过，可再生能源并不是一个简单的万能方案。每个地方都需要考虑天气、地理条件、现有电网以及居民的需求。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
One challenge is that the sun does not always shine and the wind does not always blow. Energy storage can help by saving extra electricity for times when production is low. Better power lines can also move electricity from sunny or windy areas to cities that need it. Homes and businesses can contribute by improving insulation and using efficient lights and appliances. Saving energy is often cheaper than producing more of it. A mix of different sources, storage, and careful planning makes the whole system more dependable.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
一个挑战是，太阳并非一直照耀，风也不总是吹。储能可以把多余的电力保存下来，在发电量较低时使用。更完善的输电线路也能把阳光充足或风力强劲地区的电力输送到有需要的城市。家庭和企业可以通过改善隔热、使用节能灯和电器来出一份力。节约能源往往比增加发电量更便宜。多种能源、储能设施和周密规划相结合，能让整个系统更加可靠。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
The transition also affects workers and communities. New projects can create jobs, but changes in older energy industries may leave some workers needing support or new training. Local people should have a voice when a large project is planned near their homes. Governments can help by setting clear rules, improving public transport, and supporting research. Individuals cannot rebuild the energy system alone, yet they can ask where their electricity comes from and support practical improvements. A cleaner future depends on technology, fair planning, and steady cooperation.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
能源转型也会影响劳动者和社区。新项目能够创造就业机会，但传统能源行业发生变化时，一些劳动者可能需要支持或接受新的培训。在居民区附近规划大型项目时，当地人应该有表达意见的机会。政府可以通过制定明确规则、改善公共交通和支持研究来提供帮助。个人无法独自重建能源系统，但可以了解电力来源，并支持切实可行的改进。更清洁的未来依赖技术、公平规划和持续合作。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Energy choices can also affect household bills. Better insulation, smart controls, and efficient appliances may lower the amount of power a home needs. Support is important for renters and low-income families, who may not be able to pay for improvements upfront.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
能源选择也会影响家庭账单。更好的隔热、智能控制设备和节能电器可能减少住宅的用电量。对租房者和低收入家庭来说，相关支持很重要，因为他们可能无力提前支付改造费用。

## 15. Why Museums Still Matter

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Museums are sometimes described as quiet rooms full of old things, but a good museum is more like a place where people ask questions. An object can show how a family cooked, how a city traded, or how people responded to a major change. Labels and photographs provide useful facts, while the objects themselves make history feel close and real. Visitors do not need to know a great deal before they arrive. Curiosity is enough to begin. A small tool or handwritten letter can lead to a much larger story about work, migration, invention, or everyday life.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
博物馆有时被描述为摆满旧物的安静房间，但好的博物馆更像是一个让人提出问题的地方。一件物品可以展现一个家庭如何做饭、一座城市如何贸易，或人们如何应对重大变化。说明文字和照片提供有用的事实，而物品本身则让历史变得亲近、真实。参观者不必事先懂很多知识，只要有好奇心就能开始。一件小工具或一封手写信，都可能引出有关工作、迁徙、发明或日常生活的大故事。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Museums can also help people understand different points of view. An exhibition about a difficult period should not hide disagreement or present one group as the only voice. Personal stories can show how a large event affected children, workers, newcomers, and families in different ways. Visitors may leave with questions rather than a single easy answer. That is not a failure. Learning history means examining evidence and noticing whose experiences were recorded and whose were left out. Thoughtful museums make room for discussion and invite people to keep learning after they go home.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
博物馆还能帮助人们理解不同观点。介绍艰难时期的展览不应掩盖分歧，也不应把某一个群体呈现为唯一的声音。个人故事可以展示重大事件以不同方式影响了儿童、工人、新移民和各个家庭。参观者离开时也许会带着问题，而不是得到一个简单答案，这并不代表失败。学习历史意味着检查证据，并留意哪些人的经历被记录下来、哪些经历被遗漏。用心设计的博物馆会为讨论留出空间，也会鼓励人们回家后继续学习。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Modern museums are changing how they welcome the public. Some offer audio guides, hands-on activities, quiet hours, or free days for local residents. Online collections allow people to explore objects even when they cannot travel. These tools are valuable, but they work best when they support human connection rather than replace it. A museum visit can bring generations together: a grandparent may explain an old photograph, while a child notices a detail adults missed. By preserving objects and sharing many stories, museums help communities think about where they came from and what they want to remember.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
现代博物馆正在改变迎接公众的方式。有些博物馆提供语音导览、互动活动、安静时段，或面向当地居民的免费开放日。线上馆藏让无法出行的人也能探索展品。这些工具很有价值，但最好用来促进人与人的交流，而不是取代交流。参观博物馆可以让不同世代聚在一起：祖父母也许会讲解一张老照片，孩子则可能发现成年人忽略的细节。博物馆保存物品、分享多种故事，帮助社区思考自己的来处，以及希望铭记什么。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Museums can make learning welcoming by offering clear explanations in more than one language and providing places to sit. A good visit does not depend on reading every label. Choosing one object and asking what it reveals can be enough to make the experience meaningful.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
博物馆可以通过提供多语言的清晰说明和休息座椅，让学习体验更加友好。一次好的参观不必读完每一块说明牌。只要选一件展品，思考它能告诉我们什么，就足以让这次体验变得有意义。

## 16. Keeping Friendships Alive as Adults

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Making friends as a child can happen naturally because classmates see one another every day. Adult life is different. People change jobs, move to new neighborhoods, care for relatives, and follow busy schedules. Even people who value friendship may go weeks without speaking to someone they like. This does not always mean the friendship is fading; sometimes both people are simply tired. Still, close relationships need attention. A short message, a shared walk, or a planned lunch can remind a friend that they matter.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
孩子交朋友可能很自然，因为同学每天都能见面。成年后的生活则不同。人们会换工作、搬到新的社区、照顾亲人，还要应付忙碌的日程。即使很重视友情，也可能好几个星期都没有联系喜欢的朋友。这并不一定意味着友情正在淡去，有时只是双方都很疲惫。不过，亲密关系需要用心维护。一条简短的信息、一次散步或约好的午餐，都能让朋友知道自己很重要。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Good friendship does not require constant contact or expensive plans. Some friends talk for hours, while others feel comfortable sharing a quiet meal. What matters is respect and interest. Ask questions and listen to the answer instead of waiting only for your turn to speak. Remember important events, but understand that people may not reply immediately. If a misunderstanding occurs, a direct and kind conversation is often better than guessing what the other person meant from a short text. Honest communication can prevent a small worry from growing into distance.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
维持友情不需要不停联系，也不需要昂贵的安排。有些朋友可以聊上几个小时，另一些朋友则觉得安静地一起吃顿饭就很舒服。重要的是尊重和关心。提出问题，并认真听对方的回答，而不是只等着轮到自己说话。记住重要的日子，也要理解别人可能无法马上回复。如果发生误会，坦诚而友善地谈一谈，往往比仅凭一条简短短信猜测对方的意思更好。真诚沟通可以避免小小的担忧逐渐变成疏远。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Friendships also change over time. Two people may share an interest for years and later develop different routines. They can stay connected by finding new activities, accepting a slower rhythm, or simply checking in now and then. It is healthy to have more than one source of support, so no single friend has to meet every emotional need. Friendship is not measured by the number of photographs together or how quickly someone answers. It is built through trust, kindness, and the feeling that both people can be themselves.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
友情也会随着时间改变。两个人可能多年共享一种兴趣，后来却形成了不同的生活节奏。他们可以一起寻找新的活动、接受联系频率变低，或只是偶尔问候彼此。拥有不止一个支持来源是健康的，这样就不会要求某一位朋友满足所有情感需求。友情不是用合照数量或回复速度来衡量的，而是通过信任、善意，以及双方都能做真实的自己来建立。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
When friends live far apart, a regular call can be easier to keep than a vague promise to meet soon. Shared calendars help busy people find a time, and voice messages can make a quick check-in feel more personal. Simple, consistent effort often matters more than a grand gesture.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
朋友住得很远时，定期通话往往比含糊地约定“改天见面”更容易坚持。共享日历能帮助忙碌的人找到合适时间，语音消息也能让简单的问候显得更亲切。简单而持续的付出，往往比盛大的举动更重要。

## 17. Learning New Things at Any Age

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
People sometimes think learning belongs mainly to childhood and school. In reality, adults learn throughout life, often because their needs change. Someone may study a new computer program for work, learn to cook after moving out, or take a first-aid course to help a family member. Learning can also bring pleasure. Playing an instrument, studying local history, or speaking another language can make ordinary weeks feel more interesting. The learner does not need to become an expert. A useful goal may simply be to understand more than before and feel comfortable trying something new.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
人们有时认为学习主要属于童年和校园生活。事实上，成年人也会持续学习，往往是因为需求发生了变化。有人为了工作学习新的电脑软件，有人搬出来住后学习做饭，也有人为了帮助家人去上急救课。学习也能带来乐趣。学习乐器、了解当地历史或说另一种语言，都能让平常的日子变得更有意思。学习者不必成为专家；一个有用的目标可能只是比以前多懂一些，并且愿意尝试新事物。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Adults often have less free time than students, so short, practical lessons can work well. A person might practice for fifteen minutes during lunch, follow a clear online course, or learn one task by asking an experienced coworker. It helps to connect new information with something already familiar. For example, someone learning spreadsheets can use a real household budget rather than an abstract exercise. Practice matters because reading an explanation is not the same as using a skill. Small attempts make it easier to notice what is still confusing.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
成年人通常没有学生那么多空闲时间，因此简短而实用的课程可能更合适。一个人可以在午休时练习十五分钟、跟着清晰的网络课程学习，或向有经验的同事请教一项具体工作。把新知识和熟悉的事物联系起来也很有帮助。例如，学习电子表格的人可以用真实的家庭预算练习，而不是做抽象题目。练习很重要，因为阅读说明并不等于真正掌握技能。小规模尝试能让人更容易发现自己仍然不明白的地方。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
It is normal to forget details or feel slow at the beginning. Comparing your progress with someone who has practiced for years can make learning discouraging. Instead, set a small target and record what you can do now that was difficult last month. Ask for feedback from a patient teacher or a helpful friend. If a method is not working, change it rather than deciding that you are incapable. Learning keeps people adaptable, connects them with others, and can open doors to new work or interests. Curiosity is not limited by age; it grows whenever people give it time.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
刚开始时忘记细节或觉得进步缓慢都很正常。把自己的进度和已经练习多年的人比较，可能会让学习变得令人沮丧。不如设定一个小目标，并记录现在能做到哪些上个月还觉得困难的事情。可以向耐心的老师或乐于帮助的朋友征求反馈。如果一种方法不起作用，就换一种，而不要认定自己没有能力。学习能让人保持适应力、与他人建立联系，也可能开启新的工作或兴趣机会。好奇心不受年龄限制，只要人们愿意投入时间，它就会不断成长。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Learning with other people can make a new skill easier to practice. A class, hobby group, or study partner offers encouragement and a reason to continue. People can share useful resources and celebrate small steps. The social connection may become just as valuable as the knowledge itself.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
和他人一起学习能让新技能更容易练习。课程、兴趣小组或学习伙伴会带来鼓励，也给人继续学习的理由。大家可以分享有用的资源，并庆祝一点一滴的进步。人与人之间的联系，可能和知识本身一样有价值。

## 18. Small Acts That Build a Community

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
A community is more than a group of people who live near one another. It becomes stronger when residents feel that they can ask for help and offer help in return. Small acts can create that feeling: carrying a neighbor's groceries, sharing a tool, or checking on an older person during a heat wave. These actions do not require people to become close friends. They simply show that someone has noticed another person's needs. Over time, small moments of trust can make a street or apartment building feel safer and more welcoming.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
社区不仅仅是一群住得彼此很近的人。居民觉得自己可以求助，也能反过来帮助别人时，社区才会变得更紧密。提邻居的杂货、分享工具，或在热浪来袭时关心年长者，这些小事都能带来这种感觉。这并不要求人们成为亲密朋友，只是表达有人留意到了他人的需要。久而久之，点滴信任会让一条街或一栋公寓楼显得更安全、更友好。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Shared places are important too. A clean playground, a safe walking path, or a library with welcoming staff gives people a reason to spend time together. Local events do not need to be large or expensive. A book swap, a weekend clean-up, or a community meal can bring different generations together. When residents help plan these activities, they are more likely to feel that the place belongs to them. Good planning should also make sure that people with disabilities, newcomers, and families with limited money can take part.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
共享空间也很重要。干净的游乐场、安全的步道，或工作人员热情友善的图书馆，都会让人们有理由聚在一起。社区活动不必规模很大，也不必花很多钱。交换书籍、周末清洁活动或社区聚餐，都能让不同世代的人相聚。居民参与活动策划时，更容易觉得这个地方也属于自己。好的安排还应该确保残障人士、新居民和经济条件有限的家庭都能参与。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
No community is perfect, and people will sometimes disagree about noise, parking, pets, or shared costs. Listening before reacting can prevent a small problem from becoming a lasting conflict. Clear rules help, but respect matters just as much. Residents can contact local services when an issue needs professional attention rather than trying to solve everything alone. A strong community does not mean that everyone thinks alike. It means people can discuss differences, protect shared spaces, and respond when someone nearby needs support.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
没有哪个社区是完美的，居民有时会因为噪音、停车、宠物或共同开支产生分歧。先倾听再回应，可以避免小问题变成长久冲突。明确的规则很有帮助，但尊重同样重要。如果问题需要专业处理，居民可以联系当地服务部门，而不是试图独自解决所有事情。紧密的社区并不意味着每个人想法相同，而是大家能够讨论分歧、维护共享空间，并在附近有人需要帮助时作出回应。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Trust grows when people see that help is offered without demanding an immediate reward. Even a brief greeting can make a newcomer feel included. These simple habits cannot remove every social problem, but they give neighbors a stronger starting point when they need to solve one together.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
当人们发现帮助他人并不要求立刻得到回报时，信任就会增长。即使只是简短地打声招呼，也能让新居民感到自己被接纳。这些简单习惯无法消除所有社会问题，却能让邻里在需要共同解决问题时拥有更好的基础。

## 19. Online Shopping and Local Shops

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Online shopping is convenient because people can compare prices and order products without traveling to a store. It can be especially useful for someone who lives far from a shopping area or has limited time. Customer reviews may offer helpful information, and a parcel can arrive at the door. However, pictures do not always show the true size, color, or quality of an item. Delivery can also take longer than expected, and returning a purchase may involve extra steps. A low price is not always the best value if the product does not meet the buyer's needs.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
网上购物很方便，因为人们不用去商店就能比较价格并下单。对于住得离购物区较远或时间有限的人来说，它尤其有用。顾客评价可能提供有帮助的信息，包裹也能送到家门口。不过，图片并不总能准确呈现商品的尺寸、颜色或质量。配送时间也可能比预期更长，退货还可能需要额外步骤。如果商品不符合需求，低价并不一定代表更划算。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
Local shops offer a different experience. Customers can see and touch many products before buying, ask a staff member a question, and take the purchase home immediately. A small shop may also support local jobs and give a neighborhood its character. Prices can sometimes be higher, and the selection may be smaller than a large website. Still, a local seller might offer repairs, personal advice, or products made nearby. Choosing a local shop for some purchases can keep money circulating in the community.

**中文 — 第 2 段**  
本地商店提供的是另一种购物体验。顾客购买前可以查看、触摸许多商品，向店员提问，也能马上把商品带回家。小商店还可能支持本地就业，并为社区带来独特风貌。价格有时会高一些，商品选择也可能不如大型网站丰富。不过，本地商家可能提供维修、个性化建议或附近生产的商品。部分消费选择本地商店，能让资金继续在社区内流动。

**EN — Paragraph 3**  
Shoppers can choose the method that fits the purchase. A standard household item may be easy to order online, while shoes or fresh food may be better to inspect in person. Before buying, check the total cost, delivery time, return policy, and whether the seller is trustworthy. Avoid purchasing more than you need just because an offer is available. Shopping wisely is not about always choosing the cheapest option or rejecting technology. It is about balancing convenience, quality, cost, and the effect a choice has on the people around you.

**中文 — 第 3 段**  
购物者可以根据商品选择合适的方式。普通家用品可能很适合网上订购，而鞋子或新鲜食品则更适合当面查看。购买前要确认总价、配送时间、退货政策，以及商家是否可信。不要仅仅因为有优惠就买超过所需的东西。理性购物并不意味着总选最便宜的商品，也不是拒绝使用科技，而是在便利、质量、价格和选择对周围人产生的影响之间取得平衡。

**EN — Paragraph 4**  
Businesses can also adapt by offering online ordering with local pickup or by sharing clear information about product care. Such services combine digital convenience with local contact. Customers benefit when sellers make costs, policies, and delivery promises easy to understand.

**中文 — 第 4 段**  
商家也可以通过提供网上下单、到店取货，或清楚说明商品护理方式来适应变化。这类服务把数字化便利和本地交流结合起来。商家让价格、政策和配送承诺都容易理解时，顾客也会受益。

## 20. Getting Ready for Severe Weather

**EN — Paragraph 1**  
Severe weather can arrive quickly, and a little preparation can reduce confusion. The most useful information is local: people should know whether their area is at risk of flooding, wildfire, extreme heat, or strong storms. Follow official weather alerts instead of relying only on social media posts. A household can choose a safe place to meet, keep important phone numbers written down, and make a simple plan for children, older relatives, and pets. The plan should explain how family members will contact one another if phone service is interrupted.

**中文 — 第 1 段**  
恶劣天气可能很快到来，提前做一点准备就能减少混乱。最有用的是当地信息：人们应该了解自己所在地区是否有洪水、野火、极端高温或强风暴风险。应关注官方天气警报，而不是只依赖社交媒体上的消息。家庭可以选定一个安全的集合地点，把重要电话号码写下来，并为孩子、年长亲属和宠物制定简单计划。如果电话服务中断，计划还应说明家人如何彼此联系。

**EN — Paragraph 2**  
An emergency bag does not need to be complicated. Drinking water, basic food, necessary medicine, a flashlight, batteries, a phone charger, and copies of key documents can be helpful. Check supplies from time to time and replace anything that has expired. People who need medical equipment or special food should plan for those needs first. If authorities ask residents to leave, follow their directions and use the recommended route. Never drive through floodwater; it may be deeper or faster than it appears.

**中文 — 第 2 段**

Preparation also includes looking after neighbors. Someone who lives alone may need a check-in, and a family without a car may need help reaching a safe place. After a storm, avoid fallen power lines and damaged buildings, and report serious dangers to local services. It is normal to feel worried, but clear information and practical steps can help people act calmly. No plan can prevent every loss, yet preparation gives families more choices and helps communities recover together.
